The **EU Deforestation Regulation (EUDR)** requires that coffee placed on the EU market is deforestation-free, produced legally, and backed by a due-diligence statement. For buyers of East African coffee, the key question is data.

> **Check the current timeline.** The application date has been postponed more than once. Confirm the dates that apply to your company with your legal adviser or the European Commission's EUDR guidance.

## What the regulation asks for

- **Geolocation** of every plot where the coffee was grown (points for small plots, polygons for larger ones).
- Evidence that the land was **not deforested after 31 December 2020**.
- Evidence of **legal production** in the country of origin.
- A **due-diligence statement** filed by the operator placing the coffee on the EU market.

## Why it is harder for smallholder coffee

Most East African coffee comes from smallholders who deliver cherry to shared washing stations. Linking each bag to specific plots requires farmer registries, mapping and careful lot separation.
