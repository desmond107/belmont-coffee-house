Between picking and milling, the coffee seed has to be separated from the fruit and dried. **How** that happens is one of the biggest influences on flavour.

## Washed (fully washed)

Cherries are pulped, fermented in tanks for 12 to 48 hours to break down the sticky mucilage, washed in clean water, then dried as parchment on raised beds.

- **Cup:** clean, bright, transparent; origin character comes through clearly.
- **Where:** the standard in **Kenya** and **Rwanda**, and in Yirgacheffe's best stations.

## Natural (dry process)

Whole cherries are dried in the sun, on raised beds, for three to four weeks before the dried fruit is hulled away.

- **Cup:** fruit-forward, jammy, heavier body. Strawberry and blueberry notes are classic in **Ethiopian** naturals.
- **Risk:** needs careful turning to avoid over-fermentation. Quality control matters enormously.

## Honey (pulped natural)

Cherries are pulped but the mucilage is left on the parchment while it dries. The more mucilage, the "darker" the honey.

- **Cup:** sweet, round and silky, sitting between washed and natural.
- **Where:** increasingly used in **Rwanda** for special lots.

## Which to buy?

For a **clean, classic origin profile**, choose washed. For **show-stopping fruit**, choose natural. For **sweetness and body with clarity**, try honey. Our offer list labels every lot's process, and we're happy to send a side-by-side sample set.
